The Sun is a star made of gas. It is not rocky and hard like the Earth. It is burning hot and
very bright.

The Earth moves around the Sun. Scientists call the movement around the Sun an orbit (1.
38). Everything that orbits the Sun is part of the solar system. We could not live without the Sun.
It keeps us warm and gives us light. It provides us with energy and makes plants grow.

The Sun is also like a big anchor (#4). It creates gravity that keeps all the planets moving
around it in the solar system. If there is no Sun, planets will simply fly off into the universe.

The Milky Way Galaxy (2A)

The Sun is in the Milky Way Galaxy. A galaxy is a group of stars and planets. There are
around 200 billion stars in the Milky Way. The Sun orbits the middle of the Milky Way. As it
moves, it brings the planets in the solar system along with it. The Earth goes along for the ride.
One full orbit takes 230 million years.

4.5 Billion Years Old

The Sun is about 4.5 billion years old. It will not always be there. One day it will die.
Scientists think it will last for another 6.5 billion years. As it starts to die, it will grow very large.
It will burn up Mercury and Venus. It may even burn up the Earth.

Very Hot

The Sun is very, very hot. The inside is about 27 million degrees. The outer part is about
10,000 degrees.

The Photosphere

The main outside part of the Sun is called the photosphere. It is the part we can see. The
photosphere gives off the sunlight we get here on the Earth. The Sun’s light only takes eight
minutes to reach us.

microplastics into the food we eat.

To reduce the risk of being hurt by microplastics, we should try to eat less food with plastic
packages (包装 ). We can also choose glass boxes instead of plastic ones when keeping food. Try
wearing clothes made from natural materials and using fewer plastic things. Besides, we should
sort and recycle plastic waste well to make its bad effect on our environment smaller.
